Whether you're buying your first card or listing your thousandth, understanding condition grades is the single most important skill in the TCG trading world. Here's everything you need to know.

## Why Grading Matters

A card's condition directly affects its value. A Near Mint Charizard Base Set can command hundreds of CHF more than the same card in Heavily Played condition. Misrepresenting a grade — even accidentally — destroys trust and leads to disputes.

## The Standard Condition Scale

### Mint (MT)
The highest raw condition. No visible wear whatsoever: sharp corners, perfect centering, clean surface, no scratches. Practically impossible to maintain once a card has been handled. Very rare.

### Near Mint (NM)
The target condition for competitive players and collectors. Cards may have extremely minor imperfections — a tiny edge nick or barely perceptible surface scuff — but look virtually flawless at a normal viewing distance.

**What to look for:** No creases, no scratches visible to the naked eye, good to excellent centering.

### Lightly Played (LP)
Light wear is evident but the card is still clearly well-cared-for. Expect minor edge wear, one or two small scuffs, or slight surface marks.

### Moderately Played (MP)
Noticeable wear throughout — creases, heavier edge wear, scratches. Still playable but the condition is obvious.

### Heavily Played (HP)
Significant damage: deep creases, whitening on edges and corners, heavy scratches. Usually only worth including in budget lots.

### Damaged (DMG)
Tears, holes, water damage, writing on the card. Essentially unplayable.

## How to Grade Your Cards

1. **Clean your hands.** Oils transfer to cards.
2. **Use good lighting.** Hold the card at a 45° angle to a bright lamp to catch surface scratches that aren't visible head-on.
3. **Check corners first.** This is where wear shows first. Gently roll the card in your fingers.
4. **Inspect edges.** Look for whitening or chipping.
5. **Check centering.** Hold the card at arm's length. The border should look even on all sides.
6. **Photograph everything.** Good photos protect both buyer and seller.

## Professional Grading Services

If you have a very high-value card, consider sending it to a professional grading service:

- **PSA (Professional Sports Authenticator)** — the gold standard. 1–10 numeric scale.
- **BGS (Beckett Grading Services)** — uses half-point grades, also grades subcomponents.
- **CGC** — growing quickly, competitive pricing.
- **TAG / ACE** — newer services with faster turnaround times.

Professional slabs add a premium but also guarantee authenticity and provide an independent condition assessment.

## Tips for Sellers

- **When in doubt, grade down.** Buyers are happier than expected rather than disappointed.
